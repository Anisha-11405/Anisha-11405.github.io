SmartLabel AI

A label compliance screening app. Upload a photo of a packaged product's label and it reads the text, extracts the mandatory declarations, and checks them against a set of preliminary rules. Every result shows the evidence found and what to do next.

What it does

  • Accepts a label image of up to 10 MB with a live preview, and reads it with Tesseract OCR on the server.
  • Extracts 15 declarations with pattern matching: product and common name, manufacturer, packer, importer, country of origin, net quantity, MRP, batch or lot number, manufacturing and packing dates, best before, consumer care details, unit sale price and product dimensions.
  • Runs 11 explainable checks. Each records its requirement, legal reference, the evidence found and the action to take, and returns Pass or Review.
  • Calculates a compliance score from the share of checks passed. Anything software cannot judge, such as legibility and prominence, goes to manual verification instead of being passed automatically.
  • Saves every inspection to MySQL with a readable ID such as SL-000123 so it can be reopened later.
  • Offers a dashboard with totals by status and the average score, a history view with search, filter and delete, printable and JSON exports, a demo mode, and a layout that works on mobile.

How an inspection runs

  1. Upload. The React client sends the label image, product category and package type to the API as multipart form data.
  2. OCR. The service passes the image to Tesseract and collects the recognised text.
  3. Extraction. Regular expressions pull each declaration out of the text.
  4. Rule evaluation. The 11 checks run against the extracted values and produce a score and status.
  5. Saving. The inspection is stored through Spring Data JPA and the full report is returned to the client.
